Privacy

Who we are

MOTN is made by Elias Hopman in Sweden, who is responsible for your data. Questions and requests: eliazhopman@gmail.com.

What we store

Your account email, and what you log in MOTN: food, water, weight, measurements, progress photos, fasts, phases and check-ins.

Why

To run the app for you: your plan, your targets and your history on every device. Food, weight and body data count as health data, so we only store them because you create an account and log them yourself.

Where

On your phone, and in our database at Supabase in London, so your data follows you to a new device. Only you can read your data; every row is locked to your account.

Apple Health

If you connect it, MOTN reads your weight and active energy and writes the food, water and weight you log. Weights you import are synced with your account like the ones you enter. Health data is never used for advertising or shared.

Food lookups

Searches and barcode scans are sent to Open Food Facts to find products. They are not linked to your account.

AI logging

Log with AI sends your text or photo, through our server, to Anthropic, our AI provider in the US, to estimate the food. Nothing else about you is sent with it, MOTN doesn't keep the photo, and Anthropic doesn't use it to train its models. MOTN asks before the first time.

Outside the EU

The UK, where our database is, is recognised by the EU as protecting data to the same standard. Sending AI requests to the US is covered by the EU's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Payments

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your card details.

How long

As long as you have an account. When you delete it, your data is removed from our servers straight away, and from backups within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, have it corrected or deleted, or object to how it's used: email eliazhopman@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

What we don't do

No ads. No selling your data, and no sharing beyond the services named here. No tracking across apps or websites.

Delete everything

You → Delete account removes your account, every synced row and your photos from our servers, and clears the phone.
